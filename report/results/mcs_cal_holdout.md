# Hold-out test of the MCS calibration of exiting muon candidates

Generated by `scripts/mcs_cal_holdout.py` from the control-region candidates of `scripts/mcs_cal_derive.py` (beam-on data against overlay, beam-off and dirt, all opened control regions). The nominal quantile maps are derived on one part of the candidates and tested on the other; "release" applies the maps derived on all candidates (in-sample for the test events). p_mu bins: data / prediction in the p_mu bins of version 1.8 (the two highest are one bin from version 1.9), shape; |dir x| and length: data / prediction of the fraction of exiting candidates with an MCS momentum above 1.2 GeV/c; chi2: shape chi2 of the released p_mu bins (5 dof) and of the MCS spectrum of exiting candidates.
