## The Width-Aware Regularisation Fix

The underlying cause of the model collapse in the angular observables was a mismatch between the mathematical operator and the physical binning.

* The original Wiener-SVD regularisation matrix utilized a standard tridiagonal stencil (1, -2, 1).


* This specific operator inherently assumes a uniform grid, but the analysis bin widths varied drastically, reaching ratios up to 14:1 for $cos~\theta_{\mu}$.


* Applying a uniform operator to highly non-uniform bins caused the regularisation matrix to become near-singular.
